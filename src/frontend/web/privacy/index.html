Privacy Policy (GDPR & CCPA Compliant)

Last Updated: August 30, 2026 • freeOCR.me Trust & Ephemeral Architecture

1. Zero Persistent Document Storage Guarantee

freeOCR.me processes all uploaded PDF and image files strictly in volatile Linux RAM disk (tmpfs). Uploaded documents are automatically unlinked and purged permanently from memory immediately upon conversion completion or within 24 hours. We never inspect, store, mine, or retain your documents on persistent hard drives or external storage volumes.

2. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users possess explicit rights regarding personal data processing. freeOCR.me operates on a strict zero-registration, privacy-first model: we do not collect personal identification, user accounts, passwords, or stored file data. Any user interactions occur anonymously without identity correlation.

3. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Disclosure

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. freeOCR.me DOES NOT SELL your personal information or document content to third parties under any circumstances.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display non-intrusive advertisements that support the infrastructure costs of running high-performance GPU and CPU optical character recognition. Google and its advertising partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve relevant advertisements based on visits to this and other websites across the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings portal or through browser-level tracking protections.

5. Anonymized Telemetry & Local Browser Storage

We collect aggregated, anonymized interaction events (such as page views and conversion success status) via Google Analytics 4 (GA4) solely to monitor server health and uptime. We utilize local browser storage (LocalStorage) exclusively to store your Light/Dark Mode theme preference and your active session limit boost passes.
