ABOUT FREEOCR.ME

Democratizing Document OCR with Zero-Storage Privacy

A high-performance web utility converting scanned documents into searchable PDFs and clean digital text without accounts, paywalls, or privacy trade-offs.

💡 1. Our Mission & Philosophy

Traditional online OCR tools typically lock users behind steep monthly subscriptions, enforce arbitrary 5-page conversion caps, or compromise document privacy by retaining user files on permanent disk storage.

freeOCR.me was designed from the ground up as a pure utility: fast, completely free, and strictly private. Anyone can upload scanned PDF documents up to 100 MB right out of the box, with optional rewarded video extensions up to 1,000 MB (1 GB) for heavy multi-hundred-page documents.

🛡️ 2. Ephemeral RAM-Disk Security (Zero Retention)

Privacy is the central architectural pillar of freeOCR.me:

  • Volatile Memory Processing: Uploaded documents reside exclusively in ephemeral Linux tmpfs RAM disks rather than persistent solid-state or hard drives.
  • Instant Cleanup: Files are automatically and irrevocably unlinked and wiped immediately upon download completion or after session expiration.
  • Zero Account Creation: No signups, passwords, cookies for profiling, or credit card requirements.

⚙️ 3. Open-Source AI Engines

freeOCR.me brings together industry-leading open-source machine learning and PDF engineering libraries:

  • Baidu Unlimited OCR (PaddleOCR): Industrial-grade deep learning model delivering high recognition accuracy across complex multilingual document layouts.
  • OCRmyPDF: The standard in PDF remastering, creating ISO-compliant searchable PDF/A documents with invisible text overlays.
  • Tesseract OCR: The proven optical character recognition engine maintained by Google and the open-source community.
  • PyMuPDF: Lightning-fast PDF parsing and rendering.

🌱 4. Transparent Monetization & Sustainability

To keep freeOCR.me permanently free for users globally without charging subscription fees or selling user data, we sustain our server infrastructure through:

  • Google AdSense: Minimal, non-intrusive display advertisements placed in designated non-obstructive page sections.
  • Rewarded Quota Extensions: Users processing large files can watch an optional 15-second sponsor video to earn +50 MB extra capacity up to 1 GB.
